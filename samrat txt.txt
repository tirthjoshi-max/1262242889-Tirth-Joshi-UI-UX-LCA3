Alright, checking out the interface here. First impression: the layout is pretty solid. I like how you placed the search bar right at the top center with the quick keyboard shortcut indicator. That’s super handy for quickly hopping between tracks without clicking around too much. The banner section for listening stats like "1,284 minutes together" with your top artist is a nice touch—it feels like Spotify Wrapped, but available anytime on your dashboard.

Moving down to the "Top tracks" section in the second view, the list layout is very clean. Having the track number, album artwork, title, artist, duration, and three-dot action menu neatly aligned makes scanning through top music effortless. Also, the big featured card on the right for "MELANCHOLY 24/7 After dark" with the live radio tag looks awesome visually. It gives the app a dynamic feel, like there's actual curated content waiting for you.

My main critique would be contrast in certain areas. Some of the muted grey text under playlist names like "Sunday sunlight" in the sidebar is slightly faint. Increasing the contrast just a little bit would improve readability, especially if someone is using this on a screen with lower brightness.
