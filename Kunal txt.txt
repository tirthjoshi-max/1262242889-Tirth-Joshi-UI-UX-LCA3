Um, okay, so initial thoughts on this interface... it looks super sleek, honestly. I really love the dark aesthetic with that muted coral accent color—it feels very modern, almost like a mix between Spotify’s clean layout and Apple Music’s slick hero banners. That main hero card that says "YOU LIKE IT AFTER DARK" with Jordan Ward listed as the top artist is really cool. It instantly feels personalized and gives off a chill, late-night vibe, which fits the name Melancholy perfectly.

The left sidebar is super clear, too. Having direct access to Liked Songs, Albums, and custom playlists like "Blue hour" or "Sunday sunlight" right under "Your Library" makes total sense. I usually get lost in Amazon Music’s UI because it feels too cluttered, but this keeps things minimal and easy to navigate. 

If I had one minor issue, it’s probably the bottom player bar. The play/pause button and control icons are nice, but the song progress slider line feels a bit too thin on my display. I feel like if I tried to scrub through a track quickly, I might miss the bar or struggle to drag it precisely. Other than that, I’d definitely use this daily.
