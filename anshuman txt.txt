Hey! So I’m reviewing the GUI for this music app. Visually, this looks super professional and polished—seriously top-tier work. The logo top-left with the waveform graphic and the name "MELANCHOLY" establishes a distinct brand identity immediately. I really appreciate how structured the side navigation bar is; dividing it clearly into "Menu," "Your Library," and "Playlists" keeps the user flow seamless.

The feature I like most has to be the featured "Live Radio" card on the second screenshot. That live video background thumbnail with the play button overlay makes the platform feel alive, almost like Twitch or YouTube Music integrated directly into a standard streaming layout. The "Trending Now" top tracks section right beside it is super clean as well.

As far as suggestions go, the horizontal volume slider or additional audio control settings seem missing or hidden on the right side of the bottom player bar. Usually, having a visible volume bar next to the playback controls is super crucial for quick adjustments. Also, adding quick action icons like queue view or device casting right next to the player control bar would complete the entire desktop streaming experience nicely.
