So checking out this frontend design... honestly, it looks really sleek and modern! I love how intuitive it feels right off the bat. If you use Spotify or Amazon Music regularly, you can transition to this effortlessly because the placement of elements feels super natural. The left sidebar layout with Liked Songs, Albums, and custom playlist shortcuts is exactly what I look for in a music web app.

The personalization touch in the hero banner—showing "Jordan Ward is your #1 artist" alongside specific listened minutes—is fantastic. It gives the dashboard a tailored, personal feeling as soon as you log in. I also really like the mini animated equalizer icon next to "Never Enough" in the top tracks list to show which song is currently playing; that’s a nice subtle micro-interaction.

My only critique would be the spacing around the main search bar at the top center. It feels slightly floating and disconnected from the rest of the header layout. Pulling it down slightly or giving it a subtle border stroke might make it blend better with the dark aesthetic. Overall, though, the design is super clean, stylish, and functional for daily listening!
